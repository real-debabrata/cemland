Campus Events is a simple event-management app for campus teams. Sign-ups, tickets, tasks and payments live in one place, so nobody is chasing spreadsheets the night before.

See what it does

What it does

Everything a campus event needs, from the first sign-up to the final bill.

  • Registration

    An online sign-up page you can share as a link or a QR code, with a waitlist and approvals.

    sign-up page, waitlist, approvals

  • Tickets and check-in

    Every attendee gets a QR ticket. Scan it at the gate; check-in keeps working offline once the event is open.

    QR tickets, gate scanning, offline

  • Email

    Send each attendee their ticket or an invitation straight from the app.

    tickets, invitations

  • Tasks and team

    Task groups, a shopping list, roles and permissions, and a chat for each event.

    tasks, roles, chat

  • Money

    Track cash and UPI payments, income, bills, vendors and your budget.

    payments, UPI, budget, vendors

  • Export and import

    Export to CSV or ZIP, and import your attendee list from a spreadsheet.

    CSV, ZIP, spreadsheets

Set it up once, share the code, and your whole team runs the same event from the same place.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Use your Google account or an email address.

  2. 2

    Create your event

    Tap New event, fill in the details and publish.

  3. 3

    Bring in your team

    Share the 6-letter event code from Details so others can join.
